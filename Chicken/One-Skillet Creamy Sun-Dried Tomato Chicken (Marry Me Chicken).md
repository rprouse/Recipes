# One-Skillet Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Chicken (Marry Me Chicken)

This take on Marry Me Chicken sears bone-in, skin-on thighs until deeply golden, then builds a creamy, sun-dried tomato sauce right in the same skillet with garlic, Parmesan and fresh herbs, all in one pan.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 6 (7 to 9 ounce; 200 to 255 g each) bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, patted dry
- 1 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt, plus more to taste; for table salt, use half as much by volume
- 3/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 large shallot (2 ounces; 60 g), finely chopped (about 1/4 cup)
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1 teaspoon chopped fresh oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes in oil (3 1/2 ounces; 100 g), chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) tomato paste
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) dry white wine (such as Pinot Grigio or Sauvignon Blanc)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) chicken broth
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy whipping cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano (2 ounces; 60 g), plus more for garnish
- Torn basil leaves, for garnish

### 🥣 Steps
1. Season chicken thighs evenly with kosher salt and pepper. (For even better seasoning and crispier skin, let chicken stand at room temperature for at least 45 minutes, or refrigerate uncovered for up to 24 hours.) Place chicken, skin side down, into a room-temperature 12-inch skillet. Set over medium heat and cook, undisturbed, until fat is rendered and skin is light golden, 10 to 15 minutes. Continue cooking, flipping every 2 to 3 minutes, until thighs are deeply golden and cooked through to at least 175°F (80°C), about 10 minutes. Transfer to a plate. Reserve 1 tablespoon of the drippings in skillet, and discard remaining chicken fat (or save for another use).
2. Add shallot, thyme, oregano, and crushed red pepper to skillet and cook over medium heat, stirring often, until shallot starts to soften, about 2 minutes.
3. Add sun-dried tomatoes and garlic and cook, stirring constantly, until garlic is fragrant, about 1 minute. Add tomato paste and stir to coat vegetables. Cook, stirring constantly, until paste slightly darkens in color, about 1 minute.
4. Add wine and cook, using a wooden spoon to scrape up any flavorful bits from the bottom of the skillet, until slightly reduced, about 1 minute. Stir in broth and bring to a simmer. Cook until reduced by half, about 4 minutes.
5. Whisk cream into sauce and bring to a simmer over medium heat. Simmer, whisking constantly, until sauce begins to thicken, about 2 minutes. Remove skillet from heat and whisk in parmesan until melted and creamy, and sauce is thickened. Season sauce to taste with kosher salt and pepper, if needed.
6. Nestle cooked chicken skin side up into sauce. Garnish with more parmesan and fresh basil, and serve.

> [!tip] Tip
> Starting the chicken skin side down in a cold skillet and letting the fat render slowly, undisturbed, is what gets the skin deeply golden and crisp without drying out the meat.
