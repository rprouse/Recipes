# Chicken Shawarma Sheet-Pan Dinner

This weeknight-friendly, halal cart-inspired dinner roasts spiced chicken thighs on a sheet pan alongside crisp chickpeas and charred red onion and bell peppers, then finishes with a lemony tahini-yogurt sauce, rice, and fresh herbs.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon ground coriander
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more for serving
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 3/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 11 tablespoons (165 ml) extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest, plus 5 tablespoons (75 ml) fresh lemon juice from 2 large lemons, divided, plus wedges for serving
- 3 1/2 teaspoons (10 1/2 g) Diamond Crystal kosher salt, divided; for table salt, use half as much by volume or the same weight
- 3 medium cloves garlic, minced, divided
- 6 (6-ounce, 170 g each) bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs, patted dry
- 1 medium red onion (8 ounces; 227 g), cut into 1-inch-thick wedges
- 1 (15-ounce; 425 g) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) full-fat strained (Greek-style) yogurt
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) tahini
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) honey
- 2 small red bell peppers (8 ounces; 227 g), sliced 1/4-inch thick
- Halal cart-style yellow rice or plain rice, for serving
- Fresh tender herbs such as flat-leaf parsley, mint, and/or cilantro, for serving

### 🥣 Steps
1. In a small bowl, whisk together coriander, cumin, paprika, black pepper, cardamom, turmeric, and cinnamon; set spice mixture aside. In a large bowl, whisk together 6 tablespoons oil, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 2 teaspoons kosher salt, half the garlic, and 2 tablespoons reserved spice mixture. Add chicken and toss until evenly coated. Cover and let stand at room temperature for 30 minutes or refrigerate for up to 24 hours.
2. When ready to roast, adjust oven racks to upper and lower-middle positions and preheat oven to 475°F (245°C). On a rimmed baking sheet, arrange chicken, skin-sides up, leaving edges of baking sheet empty. In a large bowl, combine onion, chickpeas, 2 tablespoons oil, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1 tablespoon reserved spice mixture and toss until evenly coated. Arrange onion and chickpeas evenly around chicken; do not wash bowl. Roast on lower rack until chicken and onion are starting to brown, about 18 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, whisk together yogurt, tahini, honey, 2 tablespoons oil, remaining garlic, 1 teaspoon spice mixture, 1/2 teaspoon salt, remaining 3 tablespoons lemon juice, and lemon zest. Whisk in enough water to reach pourable consistency; set aside.
4. In reserved large bowl, toss bell peppers with remaining 1 tablespoon oil, 1 teaspoon spice mixture, and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt. Working quickly, remove baking sheet from oven, flip onions, and arrange bell peppers around chicken. Transfer baking sheet to upper rack and roast until vegetables are browned, chickpeas are crisp, and chicken registers 175 to 185°F (79 to 85°C), 15 to 20 minutes.
5. To serve, divide rice evenly among 4 to 6 bowls and top with chicken and vegetables, along with any accumulated drippings. Drizzle with yogurt mixture, top with herbs and additional black pepper, and serve with lemon wedges.
