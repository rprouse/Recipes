# Cottage Cheese Meatballs

Protein-rich turkey meatballs that stay remarkably tender. Cottage cheese replaces the usual egg and bread crumbs, binding the mix and adding moisture much like ricotta would. Broiling until just browned on top, then finishing them in a simmer of store-bought marinara, keeps them juicy and gets dinner done in 30 minutes. Serve over pasta, polenta, or braised beans and greens.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for greasing
- 1 pound ground turkey (at least 6-percent fat)
- ½ cup (4 ounces) whole-milk cottage cheese
- ⅓ cup chopped basil, plus 1 basil sprig (optional)
- ⅓ cup finely grated Parmesan
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- Kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal)
- 1 (24-ounce) jar marinara sauce

### 🥣 Steps
1. Arrange an oven rack near the broiler and set the broiler to high. Add the olive oil to a large, broiler-safe skillet and swirl it around. In a medium bowl, mix together the turkey, cottage cheese, chopped basil (if using), Parmesan, garlic and ½ teaspoon salt.
2. Using oiled hands, roll the meat mixture into 12 balls (about 3 tablespoons/ 1½ ounces each) and place in the skillet as you go. If the meat is not holding its shape, refrigerate for 5 to 10 minutes to firm before rolling. Broil until browned on top (but not necessarily cooked through), 5 to 8 minutes.
3. Transfer the skillet to the stovetop. Carefully pour in the marinara sauce and add the basil sprig (if using). Set over medium-high. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring gently and occasionally, until the meatballs are cooked through, 5 to 8 minutes.
