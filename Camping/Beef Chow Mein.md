# Beef Chow Mein

A little freshness turns standard ramen noodles into something worth eating, and carrots and celery last far longer in a pack than most people assume. Jerky and vegetables get a minute in sesame oil before the water goes in, then the ramen cooks in that same broth. Step 7 is the honest one: drink the liquid or drain it, your call. Ginger, soy and sesame seeds go on at the end so they stay sharp.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Baby/Shredded/Sliced Carrots - 1/2 C /35 g
- Celery Sticks - 1/2 C / 35 g
- Beef Jerky - 3.5 oz / 100 g
- Water - 8-12 oz / 250 ml
- Ramen Noodles - 2 pkg / 170 g
- Sesame Oil - 2 TB / 25 g
- Ginger Powder - 2 tsp / 5 g
- Soy Sauce - 2-4 TB / 30 g
- Sesame Seeds - 2 TB / 18 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop carrot, celery if needed
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add oil, veggies, beef.
4. Stir. Cook 1-2 min
5. Add water. Boil
6. Add ramen. Cook until done
7. Drink or Drain liquid
8. Add sesame oil, ginger, soy, sesame seeds
9. Stir. Cook 1-2 min
