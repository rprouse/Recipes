# Bangkok Bomb

Standard ramen, elevated. This one is from Outdoor Eats' "Bomb" series, where a couple of easy to find pantry ingredients turn into something light and filling enough for a thru hiker. Thai curry paste is the trick: a tube or a sachet weighs almost nothing and rehydrates into a real sauce. Rice noodles and instant rice both go in, so you get two textures out of one pot, and the lime at the end is what stops it tasting like camp food.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 24 oz / 750 ml
- Rice Noodles - 3-4 oz / 150 g
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 185 g
- Thai Curry Paste - 2 TB / 40 g
- Packaged Chicken/Fake Meat - 9 oz / 275 g
- Salt - to taste
- Lime - 1

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add water. Boil
3. Add rice noodles, curry paste, chicken, salt. Stir
4. Cook until rice noodles almost done
5. TURN OFF BURNER
6. Add rice. Stir
7. Cover. Sit 10 min
8. Squeeze lime

> [!tip] The vegan tag depends on your protein
> Outdoor Eats lists this as vegan and vegetarian because the protein line gives you a choice. Packaged chicken makes it neither; a plant-based packaged protein makes it both. Check your Thai curry paste too, since many brands include shrimp paste or fish sauce.
