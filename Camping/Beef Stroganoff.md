# Beef Stroganoff

Stroganoff is a classic dish originating from Russia, inspired by Count Pavel Alexsandrovich Stroganoff. It's a family dish.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 910 ml water
- 1 bullion cube
- 85 g beef jerky
- 4-6 mushrooms sliced
- 225 g egg noodles
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp milk powder
- 1 (1oz) Dijon mustard packet

### 🥣 Steps
1. Add beef jerky and bullion cube to water, bring to boil
2. Add mushrooms and dry ingredients except milk powder to boiling water
3. Cook for 7 to 10 minutes
4. Add milk powder and Dijon and finish cooking
