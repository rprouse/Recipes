# Steak & Pork Fajitas

No steak, no problem. The trick here is a convenience store hack: beef jerky simmered with taco seasoning and a splash of water rehydrates into fajita filling, and pork rinds melt into it to add the fat that jerky lost when it was dried. Onion and pepper go in near the end so they keep some bite. Everything on the list is available from a gas station or dollar store, which makes this a good resupply-town dinner.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Onion - 1
- Bell Pepper - 1
- Clover Valley Oil - 2 TB / 25 g
- Clover Valley Beef Jerky - 2.4 oz / 68 g
- Pork Rinds - .5-1 oz / 14-28 g
- Taco Seasoning - 1 oz / 28 g
- Water - 2-4 oz / 60-120 ml
- Tortillas, flour or corn - 4-8
- Clover Valley Grated Cheese, Mexican Blend - 1/2 C / 35 g

**Optional:**
- Refried beans - 10-15 oz / 300-430 g
- Rice, precooked - 8 oz / 250 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Slice onion and pepper
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add oil, jerky, pork rinds, taco seasoning and water
4. Stir. Cover. Cook 5 min until soft
5. Add onion, peppers. Stir. Cook 2-3 min
6. Reserve filling. Toast tortillas over burner or in skillet
7. Fill and layer tortillas. Wrap it up!

> [!tip] Clover Valley is a store brand
> This recipe was published in partnership with Dollar General, so several ingredients carry their Clover Valley label. Any oil, jerky or grated cheese works exactly the same.
