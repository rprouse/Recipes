# Jamaican Jerk Chicken with Coconut Mango

Dried mango and coconut milk powder are the two ingredients doing the work here. The mango softens into the sauce and turns sweet against the jerk seasoning, and the coconut powder gives you a creamy curry-like base for about 20 g of pack weight. Everything goes in one pot, the rice finishes off the heat, and a pinch of sumac at the end adds the sour note that would otherwise need a lime. Comes in at 150 g a serving.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Dried Mango - 1.5 oz / 45 g
- Packaged Chicken - 5 oz / 150 g
- Coconut milk powder - 1/4 C / 20 g
- Jamaican Jerk Seasoning - 1 TB / 5 g
- Salt - to taste
- Water - 8-10 oz / 250 ml
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 165 g
- Sumac - 1/2 tsp / 2 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop/Rip mango
2. TURN BURNER ON: MED HEAT
3. Add mango, chicken, coconut, jerk seasoning, salt, water
4. Stir. Bring to boil
5. Add rice
6. Stir. Cover
7. TURN BURNER OFF. Sit 10 min
8. Sprinkle sumac
