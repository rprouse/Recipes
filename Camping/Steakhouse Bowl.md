### 🛒 Ingredients
- 1 L Water
- 85 g Beef Jerky
- Pre-cooked bacon (optional)
- Spices (garlic, onion, salt, pepper)
- 6 Mushrooms fresh or dried chopped
- Brussel Sprouts, Asparagus, Green Beans, Spinach (recommended)
- 250 g Instant garlic mashed potatoes
- Bearnaise sauce mix
- 30g Fried onions

### 🥣 Steps
1. **Chop** mushrooms, veggies, if needed
2. **TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT**
3. **Add** jerky, mushrooms. **Stir**
4. **Cook** 1 min
5. **Add** water, veggies. **Stir. Boil**
6. **Add** mashed potatoes. **Stir. Cook** I min
7. **Bowl out mixture**. Empty pot
8. **Add** bearnaise sauce mix, water (re pkg). **Stir**
9. **Cook** 2-5 min until thick
10. **Spoon** over bowl
11. **Sprinkle** onions, black pepper

## 📝 Notes
- I brought too many vegetables so it made way too much. One package of potatoes and a small handful of each veg is plenty. 6 mushrooms, a half a pepper, 6 sprouts, one scallion.
