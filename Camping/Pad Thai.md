# Pad Thai

A reader recipe that earns its place in the meal plan almost every trip — light, fast, and a lot of flavour for eight ingredients. Peanut butter and sriracha do the heavy lifting, thinned with the starchy water the noodles cooked in. Optionally add in 2tsp ginger powder and 1 tbsp rice vinegar.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 32 oz / 1 L
- Thick rice noodles - 7 oz / 200 g
- Fish Sauce or Salt - 1 tsp / 5 g
- Peanut Butter, you fav - 1/2 C / 130 g
- Sriracha - 1-2 TB / 20-40 g
- Rice Vinegar - 1 TB / 25 g
- Green Onions - 1/2 bunch
- Lime - 1 pc

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: High Heat
2. + water, rice noodles
3. BOIL until soft, STIR. RESERVE 4 oz water in pot
4. CHOP green onions
5. NOODS DONE+ fish sauce/salt peanut butter, sriracha, rice vinegar. STIR
6. Simmer 2-3 min. stirring, just long enough to pull the sauce together around the noodles.
7. GARNISH with green onions, lime wedge
