# Gnocchi w Tomato Sauce

Simple and tasty, and shelf-stable gnocchi is the reason it works. The vacuum-packed kind needs no refrigeration, cooks in a few minutes, and gives you a proper potato dumpling in camp. A tube of tomato paste is the other half of the trick: concentrated, unbreakable, and it turns into sauce with nothing but the starchy water you kept back from draining. Keep about a quarter cup of that water or the paste will stay a paste.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 24 oz / 750 ml
- Gnocchi, packaged - 16 oz / 454 g
- Tomato Paste Tube - 4.5 oz / 127 g
- Italian Seasoning - 1 TB / 10 g
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Salt - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- *optional* Chili Flake - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- *optional* Parmesan Cheese - 1/4 C / 20 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add water. Boil
3. Add gnocchi. Simmer until almost done
4. Drain water, keeping approx 1/4 C
5. Add tomato paste, Italian seasoning, garlic, salt, chili flake
6. Stir, Stir. Simmer 3-5 min
7. Add cheese, if using
