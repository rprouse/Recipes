# Pad Thai – Traditional

The secret here is tamarind paste — it carries the sour-sweet backbone of real pad thai in a squeeze tube that weighs almost nothing. Rice noodles boil fast, the seasonings are all shelf-stable powders and packets, and the whole thing comes together in one pot at about 3.5 oz per serving.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 16 oz / 500 ml
- Pad Thai Noodles - 4 oz / 100 g
- Tamarind Paste - 1-2 TB / 20-40 g
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp / 3 g
- Ginger Powder - 1/2 tsp / 3 g
- Thai Chili Paste - 1 tsp / 10 g
- Sugar Packets - 2-3
- Salt/Fish Sauce - 1 tsp / 5 g
- Green Onions/Thai Basil - 1/4 C / 25 g
- Lime Juice/Rice Vinegar - 1/2 / 1 TB / 15 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add water, rice noodles
3. Boil until soft. Stir. Drain. Reserve 4 oz water in pot
4. Chop/Slice herbs and lime, if needed
5. Add garlic, ginger, chili paste, sugar, salt. Stir
6. Cook 2-3 min. Stir
7. Garnish herbs, lime wedge

> [!tip] Tip
> The original step list never says when the tamarind goes in — stir it into the reserved water at step 5 along with the rest of the seasonings, then add the lime juice or vinegar at the end so the acid stays bright.
