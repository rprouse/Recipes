# Ultimate Steak Trail Taco

Chef Corso's verdict on this one is simply "this is just the best", and it is hard to argue after a long day. Beef jerky simmered in a couple of ounces of water turns back into something close to steak, pork rinds add fat and crunch that survive a pack, and French fried onions do the work of a garnish. It wants only 80 ml of water for the whole meal, which makes it a genuinely good choice on a dry stretch. Load up the optional lime, cheese and avocado if you are car camping.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Tortillas, flour or corn - 4-6
- Water - 2-3 oz / 60-80 ml
- Beef Jerky - 2-3 oz / 70-80 g
- Bell Pepper - 1
- Pork Rinds - 1/2 C / 35 g
- French Fried Onions - 1/2 C / 40 g
- Chili Powder - 1 TB / 15 g

**Optional:**
- Lime - 1
- Cheese, your fav - 4 oz / 115 g
- Avocado - 1

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
2. Toast tortillas over flame quickly. Reserve
3. In pot/skillet, add jerky, water. Cover
4. Simmer 3-7 min until jerky is hydrated
5. Once soft, add peppers, rinds, onions and seasoning
6. Stir. Cook 2-3 min until incorporated
7. Spoon into tortillas
