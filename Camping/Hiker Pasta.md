# Hiker Pasta

Dry ingredients that pack light and keep for the long haul, boiled up into a surprisingly tasty trail dinner.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Meat snack sticks - 2 oz / 65 g
- Sun Dried Tomatoes - 1/4 C / 20 g
- French’s Fried Onions - 1/4 C / 20 g
- Mushrooms, dried - 1/4 C /20 g
- Italian Seasoning - 1 TB / 5 g
- Garlic Powder - 1 tsp / 2 g
- Chili Flake - 1 tsp / 2 g
- Salt - to taste
- Water - 8-12 oz / 400 ml
- Pasta - 4 oz / 115 g
- *optional* Parmesan Crisps - 1/4 C / 20 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Slice snack sticks/tomatoes as needed
2. Combine all ingredients except water. Stir
3. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
4. Boil. HEAT TO MED
5. Add pasta
6. Cook until soft
7. Garnish with crisps
