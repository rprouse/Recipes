# Sausage Scramble

A proper hot breakfast for 120 g a serving. Chef Corso's argument here is worth repeating: do not be afraid of the vegetarian meat options, because they keep for days on the trail and satisfy even committed meat eaters. Powdered eggs and a packet of dehydrated ground beef both rehydrate while you chop the pepper and shallot, so the actual cooking is about four minutes. The fresh vegetables are what lift it above a standard powdered-egg breakfast.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 14 oz / 410 ml
- Powdered Eggs - 4 TB / 40 g
- Oil - 1 TB / 15 g
- Red Bell Pepper - 1
- Shallot - 1
- Camp Cuisine Ground Beef - 1 pkg / 3 oz / 85 g
- Salt - 1 tsp / 5 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Hyrdrate eggs w 4 TB water
2. Hydrate ground beef re pkg. 15 min
3. Chop pepper, shallot
4. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
5. Add oil, pepper, shallot, ground beef, salt
6. Cook1-2 min
7. Add eggs. Stir. Cook 1-2 min
