# Bacon Cheddar Grits w Eggs

One of the best backpacking breakfasts going — it earns a spot in almost every meal plan. Quick grits hydrate in minutes, pre-cooked bacon and cheddar carry the fat and salt you want first thing on the trail, and the eggs cook right in the pot.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water 24 oz / 700 ml
- Quick Cook Grits/Polenta - 2 C / 350 g
- Salt - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Pre-cooked Bacon - 2 oz / 50 g
- Cheddar - 4 oz / 115 g
- Eggs or Powdered Eggs - 4 / 4 TB / 20 g
- Green Onions - 1/2 bunch

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop cheese, green onions
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add water. Boil
4. Add grits, salt, bacon, eggs. Stir, stir
5. Cook 3-5 min until hydrated, soft and eggs are cooked
6. Add cheese, green onions. Stir
