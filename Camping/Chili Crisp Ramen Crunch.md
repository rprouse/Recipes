# Chili Crisp Ramen Crunch

The lightest dinner in this batch at 100 g a serving, and one of the best. Chili crisp is the ingredient that makes it: a spoonful carries chilli, garlic, oil and crunch all at once, which is a lot of flavour for 25 g. Celery and nuts stay raw and go in after the noodles are drained, so the whole point is texture against the soft ramen. Ten minutes and one pot.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Celery Sticks - 4 / 6 oz / 160 g
- Almonds/Cashews - 1/4 C / 30 g
- Water - 8 oz / 250 ml
- Ramen Noodles - 3 oz / 85 g
- Chili Crisp Hot Sauce - 1 TB / 25 g
- Soy Sauce Packets - 1-2
- Ginger Powder - 1 tsp / 5 g
- Sesame Seeds - 1 TB / 10 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Slice/Chop celery, nuts
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Add water. Boil
4. Add ramen. Cook until done. Drain
5. Add remaining ingredients to pot. Stir
6. Cook 1-2 min

> [!tip] Discard the ramen seasoning packet
> Chili crisp and soy are the seasoning here. The sachet that comes with the noodles will just make it salty and muddy the chili crisp.
