# Not Boxed Mac & Cheese

Part of the Classics Elevated series, where a packaged standby gets rebuilt from real ingredients for about the same weight. The method that matters is step 3: drain the pasta but keep four ounces of the starchy water in the pot, because that is what lets milk powder, butter and grated cheddar emulsify into an actual sauce instead of a greasy puddle. A Dijon packet sharpens it. Make a big pot and set it up as a mac and cheese bar with different cheeses, proteins and vegetables, and it will please the whole crew.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Pasta, Mac or Small shape 1 lb -/450 g
- Water - 32 oz / 1 L
- Milk Powder - 4 TB / 30 g
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp - 2 g
- Salt - 1-2 tsp / 5 g
- Dijon mustard packet - 1
- Cheddar Cheese, grated - 8 oz / 225 g
- Butter - 4 TB / 56 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: High heat
2. + water, pasta, 1 tsp salt
3. BOIL until soft. DRAIN. Keep 4 oz water in pot
4. + milk powder, garlic powder, salt to taste, dijon mustard, cheddar cheese, butter.
5. STIR. COOK 2-4 min until melted and incorporated
