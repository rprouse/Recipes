# Chicken & Garlic Rice

Instant rice is the cheapest, most packable base in the backpacking pantry, and this is the plainest good thing you can do with it. Oil, garlic, chili flake and a packet of chicken go in with the dry rice, get a minute in the pan to bloom, then the water goes in and the burner goes off. The acid at the end matters more than it looks: a squeeze of lemon or a drizzle of rice vinegar is what makes it taste finished. Treat the ingredient list as a starting point and jazz it up however you like.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Olive Oil - 2 TB / 25 g
- Packaged Chicken - 9 oz / 275 g
- Garlic Cloves/ Garlic Pwdr - 2 / 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Chili Flake - pinch
- Salt - to taste
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 90 g
- Water - 8 oz / 225 ml
- Lemon/Rice Vinegar - 1/2 / 30 g or 1 TB/10g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop garlic if needed
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add oil, chicken, garlic, chili flake, salt, rice. Stir
4. Cook 1 min
5. Add water. Stir. Boil. Cover
6. TURN OFF BURNER. Sit 10 min
7. Fluff rice. Squeeze lemon or drizzle vinegar
