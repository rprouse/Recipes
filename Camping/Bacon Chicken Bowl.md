# Bacon Chicken Bowl

A low carb, keto-friendly trail dinner that needs no water at all: bacon, chicken, onion and a pile of grated cheddar warmed through in a single pot. Ten minutes start to finish, and because there is no rice or pasta to hydrate you can cook it with an empty bottle. Pre-cooked bacon and a chicken pouch both keep for days without a cooler, and the cheese goes in last so it melts rather than splits.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Oil - 2 TB / 50 g
- Packaged Chicken - 5 oz / 150 g
- Bacon, pre-cooked - 2-3 oz / 100 g
- Onion or Shallot - 1 small / 2
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp
- Salt - to taste
- Cheddar Cheese, grated - 4 oz / 115 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop bacon, onion
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add all except cheese. Stir
4. Cook 2-3 min until warm
5. Add cheese. Cook until melted
