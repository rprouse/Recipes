# 3 Amigos Bomb

Part of Outdoor Eats' "Bomb" series, built on the idea that three easy to find pantry ingredients can carry a whole meal. Here the three amigos are instant rice, masa, and dehydrated beans, which between them give you starch, body, and protein for about 112 g in the pack. Chili powder does the heavy lifting on flavour and the pepitas go on at the end so they stay crunchy. Filling enough for a thru hiker and cheap enough to build out of a resupply box.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 24 oz / 750 ml
- Instant Rice - 1/2 C / 75 g
- Masa/ Grits/Polenta - 1/4 C / 40 g
- Dehydrated Beans - 1/2 C / 75 g
- Chili Powder - 2 TB / 10 g
- Pepitas - 2 TB / 20 g
- Hot Sauce Packet - 2-4
- Salt - to taste

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add water. Boil
3. Add rice, masa, beans, chili powder, salt. Stir
4. Cook 1-2 min
5. TURN OFF BURNER
6. Cover. Sit 10 min
7. Sprinkle pepitas, Squeeze hot sauce
