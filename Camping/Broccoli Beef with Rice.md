# Broccoli Beef with Rice

The takeout standard, rebuilt out of jerky and a head of broccoli. Fresh broccoli travels better than people expect and it is the whole point of this bowl, so pack it for the first night out. Sesame oil and jerky go into the pot together to start, the water and instant rice follow, and ginger, soy and rice vinegar finish it off the heat. Ten minutes covered does the rest.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Broccoli - 1 C / 240 g
- Sesame Oil - 1 TB / 25 g
- Beef Jerky - 3 oz / 85 g
- Water - 8 oz / 250 ml
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 185 g
- Ginger, ground - 1 tsp / 5 g
- Soy Sauce - 2-3 tsp / 5 g
- Rice Vinegar - 2 tsp / 5 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop broccoli, if needed
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add broccoli, sesame oil, beef jerky
4. Stir. Cook 1 min
5. Add water. Boil
6. Add rice, ginger, soy, rice vinegar. Stir. Cover.
7. TURN OFF BURNER. Sit 10 min

> [!tip] Tamari for the gluten-free version
> Outdoor Eats tags this gluten free, which holds only if your soy sauce is a wheat-free tamari. Everything else on the list already is.
