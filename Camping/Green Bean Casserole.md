# Green Bean Casserole

Holiday flavours taken out on the trail, and a rare camp dish built around a fresh vegetable. Dried mushrooms and milk powder rebuild the cream of mushroom soup from first principles, a soy sauce packet stands in for the savoury depth, and a mustard packet keeps it from tasting flat. The fried onions go on last and off the heat so they stay crisp, exactly as they would on the real thing. Only 170 ml of water for the whole pot.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Green Beans, fresh - 12 oz / 340 g
- Water - 6 oz / 170 ml
- Mushrooms, dried - 1/4 C / 25 g
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Soy Sauce Packets - 1-2
- Milk Powder - 2 TB / 12 g
- Mustard Packet - 1
- French’s Fried Onions - 1/2 C / 50 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop green beans, mushrooms, if needed
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Add green beans, water, mushrooms, garlic, soy
4. Cook 7-8 min covered until mushrooms and beans are soft
5. Add milk powder, mustard
6. Stir. Cook 1 min
7. Add fried onions

> [!warning] Not gluten free as written
> Outdoor Eats tags this gluten free, but French's fried onions are coated in wheat flour and standard soy sauce contains wheat. The tag has been left off this note; use tamari and a gluten-free crispy onion if you need it.
