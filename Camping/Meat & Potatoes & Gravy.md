# Meat & Potatoes & Gravy

Called exactly what it is. Beef jerky rehydrates surprisingly well in a dish like this, because it spends long enough in the boiling potato water to come back to something like stewed beef. Real potatoes make it heavy in the pack but cheap and genuinely filling, and two packets of gravy mix thicken the lot at the end. The tablespoon of cider vinegar at the finish is not optional if you want it to taste like anything: it cuts the salt and wakes up the gravy.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 32 oz / 1 L
- Potatoes, any kind, small - 3-6 / 350 g
- Beef Jerky - 3.25 oz / 92 g
- Garlic / Garlic Powder - 2 cloves / 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Beef Gravy Mix - 2 pkg / . 87 oz / 64 g
- Black Pepper - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Cider Vinegar - 1 TB / 15 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop potatoes, garlic, jerky
2. Add water, potatoes, jerky, garlic, salt, black pepper to pot.
3. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
4. Boil until soft.
5. Add gravy mix to pot . Stir
6. Cook 3-5 min
7. Add cider vinegar

> [!note] Salt is in the steps but not the list
> Step 2 calls for salt and the published ingredient list does not include it. Season to taste, remembering the gravy mix and jerky are both already salty.
