# Chorizo Breakfast Tacos or Burritos

A filling trail breakfast for 125 g a serving, which is the kind of ratio that makes a difference on a long carry. TVP and chorizo seasoning stand in for the sausage and cost almost nothing in weight, freeze-dried beans rehydrate into something genuinely creamy, and powdered eggs go in at the end so they scramble rather than dissolve. Wrap it in whatever tortillas survived your pack: soft for burritos, toasted for tacos.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Beans, freeze-dried, flaked - 1 C / 105 g
- Textured Vegetable Protein (TVP) - 1/2 C / 55 g
- Chorizo Seasoning/Chili Powder - 2 TB / 20 g
- Salt - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Water - 24 oz / 750 ml
- Powdered Eggs - 1/4 C / 50 g
- Tortillas, flour or corn - 4-6
- Hot Sauce Packets - 2-4

### 🥣 Steps
1. Combine beans, TVP, seasonings, water in pot
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Stir. Simmer. Cook 3-6 min until soft
4. Add eggs. Stir. Cook 1-2 min until cooked
5. Toast tortillas as desired

> [!tip] Corn tortillas for the gluten-free version
> Outdoor Eats tags this gluten free, which holds on the corn side of the tortilla choice. Flour tortillas do not.
