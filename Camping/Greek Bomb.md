# Greek Bomb

Another of the "Bomb" recipes, and one of the better ones for flavour and texture. Instant rice and couscous cook in the same pot, packaged tuna carries the protein, and the olives bring salt and fat without needing a cooler. The bell pepper and lemon go in off the heat at the very end, which is what keeps this tasting fresh rather than like something reconstituted. Easy packing, and it does not need a single fresh ingredient except the pepper and the lemon.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 20 oz / 600 ml
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 150 g
- Instant Cous Cous - 1 pkg / 5.8 oz /150 g
- Packaged Tuna - 5 oz / 150 g
- Olives - 1/4 C / 50 g
- Italian Seasoning - 2 TB / 10 g
- Bell Pepper - 1
- Lemon - 1
- Salt - to taste

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop pepper
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Add water. Boil
4. Add everything, but pepper and lemon. Stir
5. Cook 1-2 min
6. TURN OFF BURNER
7. Cover. Sit 10 min
8. Add pepper. Squeeze lemon
