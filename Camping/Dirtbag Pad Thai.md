# Dirtbag Pad Thai

Pad thai built entirely out of condiment packets — ketchup and peanut butter stand in for tamarind and the sauce base, so there's nothing to buy and nothing to spoil. You can put this together literally anywhere, on any budget.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 16 oz / 500 ml
- Salt - 1-2 tsp / 5-10 g
- Pad Thai Noodles - 3 oz / 90 g
- Ketchup Packets - 2
- Peanut Butter Packets - 2
- Ginger Powder - 1 tsp / 5 g
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp / 3 g
- True Lime Packet - 1
- Peanuts/Nuts - 1/4 - 1/2 c / 25-50 g
- Sriracha/Hot Sauce - 1 TB / 10 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Add water, salt, noodles to pot
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Boil. Stir. Simmer until almost done
4. Add all ingredients. Stir. Cook 1-2 min
