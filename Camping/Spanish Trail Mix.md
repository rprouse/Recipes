# Spanish Trail Mix

Trail mix with the boring parts taken out. Dry Spanish chorizo and manchego are both cured hard enough to live in a pack without refrigeration, which is what lets this carry real savoury flavour instead of just sugar and nuts. Dates and apricots handle the sweet side, marcona almonds the fat, and smoked paprika ties the whole thing to Spain. Chop everything to roughly nut size so each handful gets a bit of all of it. Two steps, no stove, no water, 50 g a serving.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Spanish Chorizo, dry - 2 oz / 60 g
- Manchego Cheese - 2 oz / 60 g
- Dates, pitted - 1/2 C / 70 g
- Apricots, dried - 1/2 C / 70 g
- Marcona Almonds - 1/2 C / 70 g
- Smoked Paprika - 2 tsp / 2 g
- Salt - to taste

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop chorizo, cheese, dates, apricots into nut sized pieces
2. Mix all ingredients

> [!tip] Cure matters more than you think
> This works because dry-cured chorizo and a hard aged manchego are shelf stable. Soft cooking chorizo or a young manchego will sweat and spoil in a warm pack, so buy the firm, dry versions.
