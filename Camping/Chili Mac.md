# Chili Mac

Another of the Classics Elevated recipes, matching a standard packaged meal with real ingredients. Hearty and filling in the way that actually matters after a big day: pasta, beans, fake meat and cheese all in one pot. Reserving half a cup of pasta water before you build the sauce is the step people skip and regret, since it is what makes the cheddar and milk powder come together. Finish with lime juice to cut through the richness.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Fake meat, your fav - 1-13 oz / 320 g
- Water - 20 oz /560 g
- Salt - 2 tsp / 10 g
- Pasta - 1/2 lb / 225 g
- Milk powder -3 TB / 30 g
- Cheddar Cheese - 4 oz / 105 g
- Oil - 2 TB / 25 g
- Pinto Beans - 1 can (15 oz)
- Chili Powder - 2 TB / 10 g
- Lime - 1

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: Med heat
2. + water, salt, pasta. BOIL until done
3. CHOP fake meat
4. RESERVE 1/2 C water
5. + oil, milk powder, cheddar cheese
6. STIR
7. + fake meat, pinto beans, chili powder
8. COOK 3-5 min. STIR
9. GARNISH with lime juice

> [!note] A can of beans is car camping weight
> The published list calls for a 15 oz can of pinto beans, which weighs more than everything else combined. Swap in dehydrated or freeze-dried beans if you are carrying this any distance.
