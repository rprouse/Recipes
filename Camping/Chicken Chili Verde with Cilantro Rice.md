# Chicken Chili Verde with Cilantro Rice

This is the full-flavoured meal you want at the end of a long day. A pouch of salsa verde does all the work a slow-cooked chile sauce normally would, fresh Anaheim peppers give it body and a bit of heat, and the rice finishes in the residual heat while you sit down. Fresh cilantro and lime wedges go on at the table. Gluten free as written and easy to make vegetarian by dropping the chicken pouch.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Anaheim Peppers - 2
- Packaged Chicken - 5 oz / 150 g
- Salsa Verde Packet - 8 oz / 225 g
- Water - 8-10 oz / 475 ml
- Salt - to taste
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 225 g
- Cilantro - 1/4 C / 15 g
- Lime - 1

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop peppers
2. Open chicken, salsa
3. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
4. Add peppers, chicken, salsa, water, salt
5. Stir. Bring to boil
6. Add rice. Stir
7. TURN OFF BURNER. Sit 10 min
8. Add cilantro when done
9. Garnish w/ lime wedges
