# Japanese Curry & Rice – Chicken & Potato

One of the great curries of the world, and one of the easiest to carry. A single Japanese curry roux cube weighs 18 g and contains the whole sauce: fat, flour, spice and salt already blended, so it thickens on its own the moment it hits the water. A real potato is worth the weight here because it holds its shape and makes the bowl feel like dinner rather than a snack. Vinegar and soy at the end are for adjusting to taste.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 16 oz / 500 ml
- Japanese Curry Paste - 1 cube / 18 g
- Ginger Powder - 2 tsp / 5 g
- Potato, medium - 1
- Packaged Chicken - 5 oz / 150 g
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 185 g
- Rice Vinegar 1-2 TB / 20 g
- Soy Sauce Packets - 2

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop potato
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Add water, curry, ginger, potato, chicken. Stir
4. Boil. Simmer until potato soft
5. Add rice. Stir
6. TURN OFF BURNER. Cover. Sit 10 min
7. Add vinegar, soy as needed

> [!warning] Not gluten free as written
> Outdoor Eats tags this gluten free, but Japanese curry roux cubes are thickened with wheat flour and standard soy sauce contains wheat as well. A gluten-free cook needs a GF curry roux and tamari, so the tag has been left off this note.
