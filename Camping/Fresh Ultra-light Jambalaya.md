# Fresh Ultra-light Jambalaya

An absolute classic backpacking dinner. The sausage and pepper get browned properly before anything else happens, which is the step that separates this from a rehydrated rice bowl, and French's fried onions stand in for the onion half of the holy trinity at a fraction of the weight. Creole seasoning is the only spice you need to carry. Rice goes in last, the burner goes off, and ten minutes later it is done.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Green Bell Pepper - 1
- Chicken Sausage, precooked - 6 oz / 170 g
- Olive Oil - 2 TB / 25 g
- French’s Fried Onions - 1/4 C / 20 g
- Old Bay/Creole Seasoning - 1 TB / 1 5g
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 165 g
- Water - 8-10 oz / 250 ml

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop bell pepper, sausage
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add olive oil, sausage
4. Cook 3-4 min
5. Add bell pepper
6. Cook 3-4 min
7. Add onions, creole seasoning
8. Stir. Cook 1 min
9. Add water. Boil
10. Add rice. Stir. Cover
11. TURN OFF BURNER. Let sit 10 min
12. Fluff
