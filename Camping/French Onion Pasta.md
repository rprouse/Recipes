# French Onion Pasta

Rich and hearty, and exactly what you want on a cold evening at camp. This is a genuine dump-everything-in recipe: pasta, jerky, bouillon, thyme and a cup of French's fried onions all go into the pot cold, and the onions dissolve into the sauce as it simmers, which is where the French onion soup flavour comes from. Two brown sugar packets balance the salt from the bouillon. Only the parmesan and the splash of cider vinegar wait until the end.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- French’s Fried Onions - 1 C / 60 g
- Garlic Powder - 1/2 tsp / 2 g
- Thyme - 1/2-1 tsp / 1 g
- Beef Jerky - 2 oz / 50 g
- Brown Sugar Packets - 2
- Beef Bouillon Cube - 1
- Water - 16-18 oz / 500-600 ml
- Pasta, medium shape - 8 oz / 225 g
- Parmesan Cheese - 1/4 C / 10 g
- Apple Cider Vinegar - 1 tsp / 5 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Add all ingredients except cheese and vinegar to pot
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Stir. Stir. Bring to boil
4. Simmer. Stir. Stir. Cook 10-15 until pasta soft
5. Add cheese, vinegar. Stir

> [!tip] Stir more than feels necessary
> The recipe says "Stir. Stir." twice for a reason. There is barely enough water to cook 225 g of pasta, so it catches on the bottom of a lightweight pot fast, and the fried onions thicken things as they break down.
