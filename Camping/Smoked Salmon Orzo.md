# Smoked Salmon Orzo

Six ingredients, ten minutes, and some genuinely good fat, which is the thing hardest to come by on a long trip. Orzo cooks like pasta but eats like a grain, so it needs no draining skill and no cozy. The salmon goes in off the heat and only needs warming through, and the cheese and lemon do the rest. This is the fastest real dinner in the batch.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 16 oz / 500 ml
- Orzo Pasta - 8 oz / 225 g
- Salt - to taste
- Smoked Salmon - 4 oz / 140 g
- Feta/Parmesan Cheese - 1/4 C /25 g
- Lemon - 1

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add water, pasta. Boil
3. Stir. Boil until done
4. Drain pasta
5. Add smoked salmon, cheese, salt. Stir
6. Squeeze lemon

> [!note] Dairy free only without the cheese
> Outdoor Eats tags this dairy free while listing feta or parmesan. Leave the cheese out and the tag holds, and the lemon carries it fine.
