# Porcini and Roasted Garlic Stroganoff

Dried porcini are the single best flavour-per-gram ingredient you can put in a food bag, and 30 g of them will carry this whole pot. Everything except the finish goes in at once: noodles, jerky, roasted garlic cloves, mushrooms, thyme and a bouillon cube all boil together, so the pasta water becomes the sauce. Milk powder and a Dijon packet stirred in at the end stand in for the sour cream. Roast the garlic at home before you leave, since that is where the sweetness comes from.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 32 oz / 1 L
- Egg Noodles - 8 oz / 225 g
- Beef Jerky - 3 oz / 85 g
- Roasted Garlic Cloves - 6-8
- Porcini Mushrooms, dried - 1/4 C / 30 g
- Thyme - 1/2 - 1 tsp / 2-5 g
- Beef Bouillon Cube - 1
- Milk Powder - 2 TB / 18 g
- Dijon Mustard Packet - 1 / 1 oz / 31 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop/Rip jerky
2. TURN ON BURNER: MED HEAT
3. Add water, noodles, jerky, garlic, mushrooms, thyme, bouillon cube
4. Cover. Boil until almost done
5. Add milk powder, dijon mustard
6. Stir. Stir. Cook 3-4 min until desired liquid level.

> [!tip] Do not drain it
> The starch from the egg noodles is what lets the milk powder and mustard thicken into a sauce. Cook down to the liquid level you want instead of pouring any of it off.
