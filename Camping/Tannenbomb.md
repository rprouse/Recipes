# Tannenbomb

Thanksgiving dinner compressed into one pot and about 300 g a serving. Stuffing mix is an underrated backpacker staple: it is light, it cooks in minutes, and it is already seasoned, so a box of it plus a gravy packet gets you most of the way to a holiday plate. Ramen bulks it out, the cranberries and nuts go on last for tartness and crunch, and a couple of soy sauce packets sharpen the gravy. One of the "Bomb" series recipes.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water- 16 oz / 500 ml
- Stove Top Stuffing Mix - 1 pkg / 6 oz / 170 g (or similar)
- Ramen - 2 pkg / 6 oz / 170 g
- Mushroom Gravy Mix - 1 pkg / .8 oz / 25 g
- Packaged Chicken/Fake Meat - 5 oz / 150 g
- Almonds/Nuts - 1/4 C / 20 g
- Soy Sauce Packet - 2-4
- Dried Cranberries - 1/4 C / 25 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add water. Boil
3. Add ramen. Cook until done
4. Add stuffing mix, gravy mix, chicken
5. Stir. Cook 1 min. Add more water if needed
6. TURN OFF BURNER: Cover. Sit 5 min
7. Sprinkle nuts, craisins on top. Squirt soy

> [!tip] The vegan tag depends on your protein
> Outdoor Eats lists this as vegan and vegetarian, which holds only if you take the fake meat option on the protein line and check your stuffing and gravy mixes, since both commonly contain dairy or chicken fat.
