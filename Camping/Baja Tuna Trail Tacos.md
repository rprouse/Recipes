# Baja Tuna Trail Tacos

A day-hike lunch that needs no stove, no fuel, and no water. Packaged tuna, mayo and hot sauce packets, chili powder and a lime become a Baja-style filling right in the pouch or your pot, and crushed Cool Ranch Doritos go in as the crunch instead of a fried shell. Five minutes of resting makes it noticeably better if you have the patience. Heavy for its calories at 360 g a serving, but nothing here needs cooking or cooling.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Packaged Tuna - 5 oz / 150 g
- Mayo Packets - 4
- Hot Sauce Packets - 4
- Chili Powder - 1 TB / 5 g
- Lime - 1
- Salt - to taste
- Tortillas, flour or corn - 4-8
- Cool Ranch Doritos - 5 oz / 150 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. Add tuna, mayo, hot sauce, chili powder, squeeze lime, salt
2. Mix. Sit 5 min (if you can)
3. Toast tortillas
4. Add crushed Doritos
5. Wrap it up!

> [!tip] Skip the toasting to keep it stove-free
> Outdoor Eats files this under No Burner, and everything except step 3 is genuinely no-cook. Untoasted tortillas work fine, so leave the stove packed if you want the fuel savings.
