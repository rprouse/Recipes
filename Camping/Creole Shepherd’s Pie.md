# Creole Shepherd’s Pie

A good one for a group, and the creole seasoning is what lifts it out of the instant-mashed-potato rut. The order of operations is the clever part: boil the potatoes first and set them aside, then build the andouille and vegetable base in the same pot while it is still hot, and spoon the mash back over the top at the end. You get two layers out of one pot and one burner. Grated cheese between the layers is optional but worth carrying.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 16 oz / 500 ml
- Instant Mashed Potatoes - 1 pkg / 4 oz / 113 g
- Olive Oil - 2 TB / 25 g
- Chicken Andouille Sausage - 6 oz / 225 g
- Old Bay/Creole Seasoning - 2 tsp / 8 g
- Red Pepper - 1
- Fresh or Frozen Corn - 2 oz / 50 g
- *optional* Cheese, grated, your fav - 2 oz / 50 g

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add 16 oz water. Boil
3. Add potatoes. Cook re pkg instruction. Reserve
4. While boiling, Chop sausage, veggies if needed
5. BURNER: MED HEAT. Add oil, meat, seasoning
6. Stir. Cook 2-3 min
7. Add veggies, 2 oz water
8. Stir. Cook 1-2 min
9. Sprinkle cheese to top of meat mix
10. Top w/ mashed potatoes

> [!note] Dairy free depends on the mash
> The optional cheese is the obvious one, but most instant mashed potato packets contain milk solids too. Check the packet if the dairy-free tag matters to you.
