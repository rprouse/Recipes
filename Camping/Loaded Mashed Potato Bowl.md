# Loaded Mashed Potato Bowl

A great recipe with endless variation. Think freeze dried chives, French's Fried Onions, hot sauce, dried mushrooms.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 4 slices pre-cooked bacon
- Water
- Instant mashed potatoes
- 1-2 Cheese bites
- 1-2 scallions

### 🥣 Steps
1. Break up bacon and fry lightly in the cup
2. Add water an boil
3. Take off heat and add instant mashed potatoes to desired consistency
4. Break up cheese bites and mix in
5. Chop scallions and add
