# Thai Sweet Potato Soup

A hearty option for plant-based hikers that asks almost nothing of your food bag: throw a couple of sweet potatoes in the pack and the rest is powder. Coconut milk powder and ginger turn the cooking water into something like a Thai coconut broth, and mashing the potatoes right in the pot thickens it without a blender. At 425 g a serving it is the heaviest thing in this folder, but most of that weight is a vegetable you can buy anywhere.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Water - 24 oz / 700 ml
- Sweet Potatoes - 2-3
- Shallot - 2
- Coconut Milk Powder - 1/4 C / 20 g
- Ginger, Powdered - 1 TB / 5 g
- Salt - 1 tsp / 5 g
- Lime - 1

### 🥣 Steps
1. Chop sweet potatoes
2. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
3. Add water, sweet potatoes, shallot, ginger, coconut milk powder, salt until soft. 5-10 min
4. Mash sweet potatoes
5. Garnish w/ lime wedge
