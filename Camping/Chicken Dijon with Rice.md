# Chicken Dijon with Rice

A flavorful, hearty bowl that dresses up shelf-stable chicken packets with Dijon mustard, bearnaise, and a squeeze of lemon.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- Packaged Chicken - 9 oz / 275 g
- Water - 8 oz / 250 ml
- Bearnaise Sauce Packet - 1
- Instant Rice - 1 C / 150 g
- Salt - to taste
- Dijon Mustard Packet - 2
- Lemon - 1/2

### 🥣 Steps
1. TURN ON BURNER: HIGH HEAT
2. Add chicken, water, packet, salt
3. Stir. Boil
4. Add rice. Stir. Cover. Sit 10 min
5. Fluff rice. Squirt mustard Squeeze lemon. Stir

> [!tip] Tip
> You can also cook the sauce separately, before or after the rice, depending on your pot/pan situation.
