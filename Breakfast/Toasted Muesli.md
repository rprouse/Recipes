# Toasted Muesli

Muesli is granola's older, plainer Swiss relative, and the difference is entirely technical: granola is bound with oil and sugar so it clumps and lacquers, muesli is bound with nothing at all. That means the toasting has to carry the whole flavour, so the oats, nuts, seeds and coconut each go in for the time they actually want rather than sharing one tray and one timer. The reward is a jar of loose, nutty flakes that stay crisp for a month, work equally well under cold milk, hot milk, or a spoonful of yogurt, and let you taste the individual grains instead of a sugar shell. Skip the oven entirely if you like, since raw muesli is the traditional form, but ten minutes of heat roughly doubles both the shelf life and the flavour.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 4 cups large-flake rolled oats
- 1 cup rye or barley flakes
- 1 cup sliced almonds
- ½ cup roughly chopped hazelnuts or walnuts
- ½ cup raw pumpkin seeds
- ½ cup raw sunflower seeds
- 1 cup unsweetened coconut chips
- ¼ cup ground flaxseed or wheat germ
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup sultanas or raisins
- ½ cup dried apricots, chopped small
- ½ cup dried apple or dried cherries, chopped small

### 🥣 Steps
1. Heat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Spread the oats and rye flakes on a large rimmed baking sheet and toast for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring once halfway, until they smell nutty and have turned a shade darker. Tip them into a very large bowl.
2. On the same sheet, toast the almonds, hazelnuts, pumpkin seeds and sunflower seeds together for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring once, until the almonds are pale gold and the pumpkin seeds start to pop. Add to the bowl.
3. Toast the coconut chips alone for 3 to 4 minutes, watching closely, because they go from white to golden to burnt in about a minute. Add to the bowl.
4. Stir the flaxseed, cinnamon and salt through the warm mixture, then spread everything back on the sheet and leave until completely cool to the touch, about 20 minutes.
5. Fold in the sultanas, apricots and dried apple. Transfer to airtight jars and store in a cool, dark cupboard for up to 1 month, or the freezer for 3 months.
6. To serve, scoop about ½ cup per person and cover with cold milk, hot milk, or yogurt. For the softest texture, cover with milk and refrigerate overnight before eating.

> [!tip] Tip
> Treat the recipe as a ratio rather than a list: roughly 60% flakes, 20% nuts and seeds, 20% dried fruit, by volume. Anything that fits those proportions works, so buy whatever nuts are cheapest and swap freely.

> [!warning] Add the fruit only when cold
> Dried fruit stirred into warm muesli traps steam against the flakes. The mix turns leathery within a week and can mould in a sealed jar. Wait for the tray to be genuinely cool, not just warm.
