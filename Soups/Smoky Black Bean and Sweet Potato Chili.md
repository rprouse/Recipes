# Smoky Black Bean and Sweet Potato Chili

A hearty, forgiving slow cooker chili that holds happily on low for 8+ hours. Chipotle and smoked paprika bring the smoke, the sweet potatoes melt into the beans to thicken it, and lime at the end brightens everything up.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and cut in 2 cm cubes
- 2 cans black beans (540 ml / 19 oz each), drained and rinsed
- 1 can diced fire-roasted tomatoes (796 ml / 28 oz)
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 tablespoon adobo sauce
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1½ teaspoons salt
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 1 lime, juiced

**For topping**
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1½ cups shredded cheddar
- ½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped

### 🥣 Steps
1. **Load the slow cooker:** Add the onion, garlic, bell pepper, sweet potatoes, black beans, tomatoes, chipotles and adobo sauce to the slow cooker.
2. **Spice and liquid:** Stir in the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt and vegetable broth. Push the sweet potatoes toward the bottom so they sit in the liquid.
3. **Cook on low:** Cover and cook on LOW for 6 to 8 hours. It will hold fine for another couple of hours on low or warm.
4. **Thicken and finish:** Mash a few spoonfuls of sweet potato and beans against the side of the pot to thicken. Stir in the frozen corn and let it heat through, about 10 minutes.
5. **Season and serve:** Stir in the lime juice, taste and adjust the salt. Serve with the sour cream, cheddar and cilantro on the side.

> [!tip] Tip
> If the chili seems too thin when you get back, leave the lid off on HIGH for 20 to 30 minutes. One chipotle gives mild heat and two gives moderate heat. Cornbread or tortilla chips round it out nicely.
