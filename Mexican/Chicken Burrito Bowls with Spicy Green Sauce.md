# Chicken Burrito Bowls with Spicy Green Sauce

A satisfying way to pull all your favourite flavours into one colourful, crowd-pleasing dish. It starts with easy grilled chicken in a lime-and-ancho marinade and a bold, zesty green sauce you'll want to put on everything, then you riff: any grain for the base — rice, quinoa, farro, even cauliflower rice — piled with corn, beans, tomatoes, avocado, cucumbers, pickled onions, or whatever's in the fridge. Most of it can be prepped ahead, so everyone builds their own bowl at the table.

### 🛒 Ingredients

**For the Chicken:**
- 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lime zest, from two limes (save the limes for the green sauce)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1¼ teaspoons ancho chili powder
- ½ teaspoon ground coriander
- ¼ teaspoon dried oregano
- 1¼ teaspoons salt
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 2 pounds chicken tenderloins (or boneless skinless chicken breasts pounded to an even ½-inch/13-mm thickness)

**For the Green Sauce:**
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and roughly chopped (reserve the seeds; see tip)
- 1 cup fresh cilantro
- 1 clove garlic, roughly chopped
- ½ cup mayonnaise, best quality such as Hellmann's or Duke's
- ¼ cup sour cream
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice, from 1 lime
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

**For Serving:**
- Fresh corn, tomatoes, avocado, cucumbers, beans, cilantro
- About 4 cups cooked quinoa, basmati rice, or cilantro lime rice

### 🥣 Steps
1. **Marinate the chicken.** Combine all of the chicken ingredients except the chicken in a large resealable freezer bag. Close the bag and squish the marinade around to mix. Add the chicken and massage the marinade into the meat until evenly coated. Seal the bag and place it in a bowl in the refrigerator (the bowl protects against leakage); marinate for at least 6 hours or overnight, flipping the bag once or twice if you think of it.
2. **Make the green sauce.** Combine all of the sauce ingredients in a blender or food processor and blend into a smooth sauce. Taste, then blend in some of the reserved jalapeño seeds to add heat, if desired. Adjust the seasoning if necessary. Transfer to a bowl, cover, and refrigerate until ready to serve.
3. **Grill the chicken.** Clean the grill and preheat to high. Lightly dip a wad of paper towels in vegetable oil and, using tongs, carefully rub over the grates several times until glossy and coated (or spray generously with a non-flammable cooking spray). Place the chicken on the grill and cook, covered, for 2 to 3 minutes per side, turning only once — let it sear before flipping for the best grill marks and juiciest meat. Transfer to a cutting board and slice into bite-sized pieces.
4. **Assemble.** Scoop warm rice or quinoa into bowls and top with the grilled chicken, optional toppings, and green sauce.

> [!tip] Tip
> Everything through step 2 can be made ahead, which is what makes this a good dinner-party dish. After handling the jalapeño seeds, wash your hands well and avoid touching your eyes. To cook your own quinoa for the base, combine 1½ cups (270 g) pre-washed quinoa, ¾ teaspoon salt, and 2⅔ cups (640 ml) water in a medium saucepan; bring to a boil, then reduce to low, cover, and cook 15 minutes until the water is absorbed and little "tails" sprout from the grains (add 1 to 2 tablespoons more water if it's not done when the liquid runs out).
