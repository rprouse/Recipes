# How To Improve Packet Ramen

This recipe guide walks you through five different levels of improving basic packet ramen — from adding a simple whisked egg to making a rich, restaurant-style ramen with protein and homemade stock over two days. Each level builds on the last, upgrading taste and texture with easy techniques and extras that take your instant noodles to the next level.

# Level 1: Basic Ribboned or Thickened Egg Ramen

## Ingredients

- Packet ramen noodles (any brand)
- Ramen seasoning packets (included)
- 1 egg

## Steps

1. Cook ramen noodles according to package instructions.
2. While cooking, whisk egg vigorously until homogeneous.
3. Choose your preferred egg style:
   - For ribboned egg: Pour egg slowly into the hot ramen, turn heat off, cover, and let sit for 1 minute.
   - For thickened soup: Pour egg slowly while heat is still on, stirring vigorously to distribute.
4. Serve immediately.

# Level 2: Garnished Ramen Upgrade

## Ingredients

- Packet ramen noodles (e.g., Shin black ramen)
- Ramen seasoning packets
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter
- 1 egg
- Spring onion (white and green parts separated)
- Chili crisp (optional)

## Steps

1. Boil water and add ramen seasoning packets first to dissolve.
2. Stir in peanut butter for richness.
3. Add noodles and cook.
4. Whisk and add egg as in Level 1, using your preferred method.
5. Stir in chopped spring onion whites.
6. Transfer noodles and broth to a bowl.
7. Top with spring onion greens.
8. Add chili crisp if desired.
9. Serve within 4 minutes for a quick but elevated ramen.

# Level 3: Protein-Rich Creamy Broth Ramen

## Ingredients

- Packet ramen noodles
- Ramen soup base packet
- Spicy seasoning packet
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
- Spring onion whites (finely chopped)
- Protein of choice (e.g., wagyu steak, leftover roast chicken, prawns, bacon, sausage)
- Chili crisp
- Spring onion greens

## Steps

1. Prepare broth with soup base packet and water in a pot.
2. Combine spicy seasoning packet, egg yolk, mayonnaise, and spring onion whites in your serving bowl.
3. Cook noodles about 60% in the broth.
4. Take about 100 ml of broth and stir vigorously into the serving bowl mixture to create a creamy sauce.
5. Drain the noodles and mix with the prepared sauce.
6. Thinly slice and cook your protein as desired.
7. Plate noodles and top with protein, chili crisp, and spring onion greens.
8. Serve and enjoy a richer, restaurant-style ramen created in about 20 minutes.

# Level 4: Ramen Pancake (Okonomiyaki-Inspired)

## Ingredients

- Packet ramen noodles
- Reserved ramen broth
- Spring onion whites (thinly sliced)
- All-purpose flour
- Olive oil
- Protein for pancake topping (raw prawns, chicken, sausage, bacon)
- Kewpie mayonnaise
- Bulldog sauce
- Spring onion greens

## Steps

1. Cook noodles about 70%, drain and reserve broth.
2. Chill reserved broth for about 10 minutes.
3. Add sliced spring onion whites to broth.
4. Whisk in flour until batter reaches wet pancake consistency.
5. Stir noodles into batter.
6. Heat a 10-inch pan with a tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat.
7. Pour batter evenly into pan.
8. Add your protein on top.
9. Reduce heat to medium and cook until prawns are opaque or bottom is set.
10. Flip pancake carefully (using a spatula or plate if needed).
11. Cook other side until done and pancake releases easily.
12. Serve topped with Kewpie mayo, Bulldog sauce, and spring onion greens.

# Level 5: Two-Day Char Siu Ramen

## Ingredients

- Chicken frames (for stock)
- Water
- Pork belly (for char siu)
- Butcher’s string
- 3 spring onions (cut into 4cm pieces)
- 3 cloves garlic (crushed)
- Slices of ginger
- Sake
- Mirin
- Soy sauce
- 6 eggs
- 20 g sugar
- 500 ml water (for marinade)
- 80 ml Chinese black vinegar
- 200 ml soy sauce (for marinade)
- Packet ramen noodles
- Spring onion whites and greens

## Steps

**Day 1 – Prepare Stock and Char Siu**

1. Cover chicken frames with water, bring to simmer for 10-15 minutes.
2. Discard water, refill with fresh water, simmer for 3-4 hours to create clean stock.
3. Roll pork belly tightly and tie with butcher’s string.
4. Place pork on rack in oven-safe pot.
5. Add spring onions, garlic, ginger, sake, mirin, and soy sauce to pot.
6. Nestle pork into braising liquid.
7. Cover and roast in 160°C oven for 1.5 hours.
8. Boil eggs for 6.5 minutes, plunge into ice water, peel.
9. Mix sugar, water, black vinegar, and soy sauce for marinade.
10. Submerge peeled eggs in marinade, refrigerate for 2 to 48 hours.

**Day 2 – Assemble Ramen**

1. Strain chicken stock through fine sieve and refrigerate overnight.
2. Heat stock to boil, add ramen seasoning packets.
3. Add spring onion whites and cook noodles in stock for about 2 minutes.
4. Slice chilled char siu pork.
5. Place noodles in bowl, ladle broth over.
6. Top with sliced char siu, spring onion greens, and soy sauce egg.
7. Serve a luxurious, restaurant-quality ramen after two days of preparation.
