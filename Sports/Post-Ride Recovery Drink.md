Milk-based recovery shake, roughly 70 g carbs and 35 g protein. Sized for a 90-100 kg rider after a long day.

> Skip the banana and drop to 300 ml milk for a lighter version if you have a proper meal coming soon. If dairy sits badly after hard efforts, swap to 400 ml water plus 30 g whey isolate and add 200 mg sodium - you lose the casein and the natural electrolytes, but it is gentler. Pre-portion the dry ingredients into jars or bags so post-ride you only add milk.

### 🛒 Ingredients
- 400 milliliters 2% milk (or chocolate milk, reducing the sugar below)
- 25 grams whey protein isolate, vanilla or chocolate
- 35 grams white sugar (or maltodextrin/sugar mix)
- 1 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 0.3 teaspoons table salt
- 1 banana, ripe
- 30 milliliters tart cherry concentrate (optional)
### 🥣 Steps
1. Dry mix first: Combine 25 grams whey protein isolate, vanilla or chocolate, 35 grams white sugar (or maltodextrin/sugar mix), 1 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder and 0.3 teaspoons table salt in the bottom of a blender jar or shaker. Mixing the powders dry stops the cocoa from clumping.
2. Add liquid and blend: Add 400 milliliters 2% milk (or chocolate milk, reducing the sugar below), 1 banana, ripe and 30 milliliters tart cherry concentrate (optional) if using. Blend 45 seconds 00:45, or shake hard for a minute if you skip the banana.
3. Drink within the hour: Get it down within an hour of finishing, ideally within 30 minutes if you are riding again tomorrow. Follow with a real meal within a couple of hours.
### 🗒️ Notes
- Sodium citrate instead of table salt if the salty taste bothers you; it's gentler on the stomach and about 1/3 sodium by weight, so use roughly 1.7 g for the same effect
- Potassium and magnesium are mostly marketing at these timescales, but lite salt covers both if you want them
- Pre-mix dry batches in ziplocs or a jar and add water at the ride - much easier than measuring at 6 am
- Mix fresh on hot days; sugary bottles grow things fast, and rinse immediately after
- Higher concentrations (say 90-120 g in one bottle) work for a lot of people if you drink plain water alongside, but introduce it in training, never on the first day of a big block
